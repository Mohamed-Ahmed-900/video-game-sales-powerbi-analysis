# 🎮 Video Game Sales Analysis (1980–2020) | Power BI

## Overview
I built an interactive Power BI dashboard to analyze video game sales from 1980 to 2020 and used it to guide strategic investment decisions: where to increase focus and where to reduce effort.

**Business question:** Which genres, platforms, regions, and publishers are the most profitable over time, and is the market growing or shrinking?

**Tools:** Power BI · Data Modeling · Data Visualization

## Key Findings
- **Genres:** Action, Sports, and Shooter are the top three genres by global sales, well ahead of Role-Playing, Platform, and the rest.
- **Regions:** North America accounts for **49.1%** of global sales, making it the most lucrative market. The average title sells about 267K units in NA, versus 148K in the EU, 79K in Japan, and 49K elsewhere.
- **Platforms:** The PS2 leads all platforms, followed by the Xbox 360 and PS3. Sales on older platforms have declined steadily over time.
- **Publishers:** Nintendo, Electronic Arts, and Activision are the top three publishers by global sales.
- **Market trend:** Global sales peaked around 2008 and have declined since.

## Business Recommendations
1. **Prioritize Action, Sports, and Shooter games**, since they are consistently the most profitable genres.
2. **Focus investment on North America**, the largest market, while using Europe as a secondary market.
3. **Stop supporting older platforms** and shift resources to newer ones, as their sales have steadily declined.
4. **Partner with or learn from Nintendo, Electronic Arts, and Activision**, the top-performing publishers.
5. **Innovate and diversify**, since overall market sales are declining after the 2008 peak.
